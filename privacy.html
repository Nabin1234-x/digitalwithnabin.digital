🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Digital With Nabin ("we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website (digitalwithnabin.digital).

By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name: When you create an account
  • Email Address: For login and notifications
  • Phone Number: For enrollment and contact
  • Payment Proof: Screenshot when you enroll in a course
  • Address: Optional, for enrollment purposes

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, OS version
  • FCM Token: For push notifications
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process course enrollments
  • To send you notifications about new courses
  • To verify payments
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our services

Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only with:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database, and notifications
  • Google Play: For app distribution
  • Law Enforcement: If required by law

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Firebase Security Rules for database protection
  • Secure authentication via Firebase Auth
  • Regular security updates

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view your data in your Profile
  • Update: You can update your information anytime
  • Delete: You can request account deletion
  • Opt-out: You can disable notifications anytime

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Remember your preferences
  • Store cart information

Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send push notifications about:

  • New courses and PDFs
  • Enrollment updates
  • Special offers

You can disable notifications from your device settings anytime.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
