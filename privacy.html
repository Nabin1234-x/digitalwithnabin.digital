1. Introduction

Welcome to Digital With Nabin ("we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our app and services.

By using our app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (WhatsApp)
  • Address (optional)
  • Profile photo (if signed in with Google)

Payment Information

  • Payment screenshots (uploaded for verification)
  • Transaction amount and course details
  • We do not store your bank/card details

Usage Data

  • Device type and browser information
  • App usage patterns (for improvement)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for notifications

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To verify your course enrollment and payments
  • To provide access to purchased PDFs and courses
  • To send important notifications and updates
  • To respond to your support requests and chats
  • To issue certificates upon course completion
  • To improve our services and user experience

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Firebase Authentication — secure email/password and Google sign-in
  • Firestore Database — encrypted data storage with security rules
  • HTTPS encryption — all data transmitted securely
  • Access control — only admin can access enrollment data
  • Password protection — passwords are never stored in plain text

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google) — for authentication and database services
  • Payment verification — admin manually verifies payments
  • Legal authorities — if required by Nepali law

6. Your Rights

  • Correction — update incorrect information
  • Deletion — request account and data deletion
  • Opt-out — disable notifications anytime
  • Complaint — contact us with any privacy concerns

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.
