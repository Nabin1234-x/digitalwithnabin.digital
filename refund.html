💰 Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

1. Overview

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with a course, you may request a refund within 7 days of purchase as per our policy.

2. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a refund if:

  • Request made within 7 days of purchase
  • Course content accessed less than 20%
  • Technical issues prevented access
  • Duplicate payment was made

3. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be issued if:

  • More than 7 days have passed
  • Course accessed more than 20%
  • PDF books or cheat sheets already downloaded
  • Certificates already issued
  • Violation of terms (sharing content)

4. Refund Process

To request a refund:

  • Email us within 7 days of purchase
  • Include registered email and course name
  • Provide reason for refund request
  • Wait for verification (3-5 business days)
  • Refund processed via eSewa within 7 days

5. PDF Products

PDF products (Forex Guide, Cheat Sheet, Nepali Trading Book) are non-refundable once downloaded or accessed. However, if the PDF link is broken or inaccessible, we will provide a replacement or full refund.

6. Course Refunds

For video courses (Digital Marketing, Stock Market, Combo), refunds are available within 7 days if you have not accessed more than 20% of the content.

7. Fraud Prevention

We reserve the right to deny refund requests that appear fraudulent or abusive. Repeated refund requests may result in account suspension.
