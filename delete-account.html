Digital With Nabin — Account & Data Deletion

Important Warning

Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. All your course access, PDF purchases, certificates, and chat history will be permanently removed. You will lose access to all purchased content.

How to Request Deletion

Follow these steps to request deletion of your account and all associated data:

1
Send an Email

Email us from your registered email address at nabinkhanal0007@gmail.com

2
Use Subject Line

Write the exact subject: "Delete My Account"

3
Include Your Details

Mention your registered email address and full name in the email body.

4
Wait for Verification

We will verify your identity and process the request within 7 business days.

5
Confirmation

You will receive a confirmation email once your account is permanently deleted.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Personal information (name, email, phone, address)
  • All enrollment records and payment proofs
  • PDF purchases and access links
  • Certificate records
  • Chat history with admin
  • FCM notification tokens
  • Collaboration requests

Data Retention

After deletion, some data may be retained for a limited time:

  • Transaction records — 3 years (legal requirement)
  • Tax records — 5 years (Nepal tax law)
  • Backup logs — up to 30 days

After these periods, all data is permanently erased.
